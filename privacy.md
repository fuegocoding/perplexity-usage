# Privacy Policy for Unofficial Perplexity Usage & Model Watcher

**Last updated:** May 19, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the "Unofficial Perplexity Usage & Model Watcher" Chrome Extension ("the Extension"). 

## Overview
The Extension is designed to help users view their Perplexity usage limits and detect model mismatches. We are committed to protecting your privacy. The Extension operates entirely locally within your browser and does not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your personal data.

## Data Collection and Usage
The Extension requires access to certain browser features and website data to function, but **no data ever leaves your device or is sent to the developer**.

Specifically, the Extension interacts with data in the following ways:

1. **Perplexity.ai Website Data (`https://*.perplexity.ai/*`):** 
   The Extension reads the content and network requests made on `perplexity.ai` exclusively to determine your usage limits and the model currently in use. This data is processed strictly locally.
   
2. **Local Storage (`storage` permission):**
   The Extension uses your browser's local storage to save your settings, display preferences, and cached usage statistics. This data remains on your personal machine and is never synced to external servers or accessible by the developer.

3. **Browser Tabs (`activeTab` and `tabs` permissions):**
   These permissions are used to detect when you are actively using Perplexity so the Extension can trigger its analysis and update the popup interface accordingly.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure
Because we do not collect any of your data, we have no data to share. 
- We do not transmit any analytics or telemetry.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not share your information with any third parties.

## Third-Party Services
The Extension interacts with Perplexity.ai. Your interactions with Perplexity are subject to Perplexity's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. This Extension is entirely unofficial and unaffiliated with Perplexity.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time if the Extension requires new technical permissions. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of the policy.

## Contact
If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or the Extension's practices, please contact the developer via the support link provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
